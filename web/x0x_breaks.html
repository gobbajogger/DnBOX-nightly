Tips

Your loops appear as BR1, BR2 and so on, after the built-in loops. Choose BREAK → EDIT, then turn LOOP A or LOOP B.

Loops change pitch as they follow the song tempo. Use the PITCH knob to tune them.

Removing a loop moves later loops up one slot, so patterns that used them play the next loop. The manual's BREAK section

Compare before adding

Play original to hear your cut, and Play as saved to hear it the way the keyboard stores it. They play without the keyboard’s tempo or effects.
