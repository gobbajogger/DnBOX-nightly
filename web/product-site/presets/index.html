Formats & what stays unchanged

Choose the file’s original format. Synth One supports single .synth1 files and .json banks; SuperJ6 supports its bank format. Files are checked by their contents, up to 8 MiB.

Only existing preset names change here. Sequences, arp, FX, tuning, identifiers, wrappers and unknown fields stay intact. The unchanged-original download is exactly the file you opened.
