First installation and update verification

Firmware installation and bootloader adoption are different operations. Mandatory m-boot is the intended prerequisite; normal adoption is not yet available through this preview. The installer must verify the exact model and package, same instrument, complete backup and durable readback before it can offer a new adoption action.

A completed transfer or reconnect is not proof of a successful boot. Check the exact installed version and confirm controls and sound after an update. Existing user samples and projects must be preserved.
