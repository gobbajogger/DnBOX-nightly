Full control map and version-specific behavior

Play standalone

Use the firmware for your physical keyboard model. Start on the step grid, play the keys and pads, and select a part to shape its sound.

The full SMK-37 control map

Add an SMC mixer

Connect the supported SMC mixer through the keyboard’s Bluetooth MIDI setup. Use the mixer’s DAW preset. The versioned SMK-37 manual contains the matching controller, connection steps and control map.

Eight faders and encoders bring mixing, mute and solo into reach. The manual describes the firmware-specific control map.

Perform with Loopy Pro

Connect by USB to your iPad or Mac and select the keyboard’s APC40 mkII controller in Loopy Pro. KNOB BANK switches clip mode; OCTAVE− and OCTAVE+ move the pad view.

Use USB for Loopy when SMC uses Bluetooth. The roles and mappings are explained in the manual.

Installation and recovery

Back up and install or choose the recovery path for your observed state. Keep the physical model and exact package together.
